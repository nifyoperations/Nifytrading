Nify Trading Ltd. is looking for a suitable individual for the Retail Technology & Transformation Manager position for its Surrey, BC location. The role focuses on managing retail technology integration, store systems, cloud-managed infrastructure, vendor coordination, cybersecurity and digital transformation initiatives for telecommunications retail operations.

EMPLOYERNify Trading Ltd.
LOCATION15180 Fraser Hwy, Surrey, BC V3R 3P1
LANGUAGEEnglish
HOURS40 hours per week
DURATIONFull-time, permanent

Main Duties

  • Plan, organize, direct, control and evaluate the company's retail technology function supporting store operations, POS/payment connectivity, CRM/customer workflows, inventory visibility, video monitoring, secure branch operations and cloud-managed infrastructure.
  • Develop, implement and maintain policies, procedures and best practices related to retail technology infrastructure, electronic data processing, computer systems development, cybersecurity and operational support.
  • Lead digital transformation initiatives by identifying technology solutions that improve retail operations, customer experience, inventory management, sales performance and overall business efficiency.
  • Meet with internal stakeholders, vendors, consultants and business partners to determine technology requirements, define project specifications, establish budgets and develop implementation timelines.
  • Manage and coordinate cross-functional technology projects, including system upgrades, software implementations, cloud-based solutions, network enhancements and process automation initiatives.
  • Lead and coordinate internal stakeholders, external technology vendors, managed service providers, system integrators, consultants, technical support resources and project-based contractors involved in retail technology and transformation initiatives.
  • Oversee the design, development, implementation, operation, maintenance and administration of retail software applications, telecommunications systems, databases, networks and information systems.
  • Evaluate security, reliability and performance of technology systems, including secure access, branch security policy, identity-based controls, centralized monitoring and incident escalation processes, ensuring compliance with organizational standards, regulatory requirements and industry best practices.
  • Control departmental and project budgets by planning expenditures, evaluating technology investments, negotiating vendor contracts and ensuring cost-effective utilization of resources.
  • Recruit, train, mentor and supervise information technology personnel and provide ongoing professional development opportunities to enhance team performance and technical expertise.
  • Identify technology resource requirements, coordinate vendor and contractor support, provide technical guidance to store operations and support staff, and ensure knowledge transfer for ongoing technology operations.
  • Analyze business processes and operational challenges and recommend innovative technology solutions to support organizational growth, scalability and competitive advantage.
  • Assess AI-assisted monitoring, anomaly detection, video analytics, footfall visibility and store performance analytics where they can improve operational visibility and decision-making.
  • Prepare management reports, project updates, risk assessments and strategic technology plans for senior leadership, and provide recommendations regarding future technology investments and transformation initiatives.
  • Co-ordinate the work of regions, divisions or departments and report to the board of directors.

Employment Requirements

  • A diploma or above in information systems, business technology, engineering, computer science or a related field is required. An equivalent combination of senior-level enterprise technology experience and recognized industry certifications will be considered.
  • 10+ years of experience in designing and implementing enterprise-scale network and digital infrastructure solutions for multi-site organizations is required.
  • 5+ years of management or functional leadership experience in systems analysis and architecture, information systems management, network design, cybersecurity, vendor management or technology project delivery is required.
  • Industry-recognized enterprise certifications such as Cisco CCIE, CCNP Enterprise or equivalent are considered strong assets.
  • Exceptional time management skills, attention to detail, teamwork and leadership skills.
  • Ability to multitask, work independently and prioritize is required.

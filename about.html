01 / About us

About Nify Trading Ltd.

Nify Trading Ltd. is a British Columbia telecommunications retail company and authorized Freedom Mobile franchise partner operating multiple retail locations in Surrey, British Columbia.

We provide wireless plans, mobile devices, device activations, account setup, upgrades, accessories, troubleshooting, technical support and ongoing customer service.

Our goal is to provide customers with reliable, affordable and convenient wireless solutions while delivering knowledgeable and responsive in-person service through our local retail locations.

As our business continues to grow, we remain committed to improving our retail operations, customer experience and technology systems while serving communities throughout Surrey and the surrounding area.

08 / Operations

Technology & Retail Operations

Modern telecommunications retail depends on reliable and well-integrated technology systems. Across our retail locations, Nify Trading uses technology to support point-of-sale and payment processing, customer account services, inventory visibility, secure connectivity, video monitoring, store communications and operational reporting.

As our multi-location business continues to develop, Nify Trading continues to improve its technology infrastructure, security practices, vendor coordination and digital processes to provide efficient and consistent service across all locations.

09 / Our vision

Building a stronger, customer-focused telecommunications retail business.

Nify Trading Ltd. is committed to improving customer service, technology, operational efficiency and employee development while making wireless products and services accessible to the communities we serve.
